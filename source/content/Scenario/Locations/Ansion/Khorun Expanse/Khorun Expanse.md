> [!holonet] Public Holonet Entry
> The **Khorun Expanse** is a sparsely populated region located to the west of the capital of [[Ansion]], [[Cuipernam]]. While steppe is omnipresent on the planet, nowhere else does it dominate the landscape quite so completely as here. Nevertheless, smaller rivers and mountains can occasionally be found, and the region also borders the larger of the two southern seas. 
> 
> The settled population of the Khorun Expanse is largely concentrated in the trading town of [[Dashbalar]] and its vicinity, though there are several smaller farm- and homesteads to be found, tucked away between the shallow hills, where they are better shielded from the ever-present winds. While much fewer in numbers today, [[Civilians.md|Alwari]] caravans can still regularly be encountered traversing the region in seasonal intervals.
