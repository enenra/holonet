> [!quote] Description
> The trade town [[Dashbalar]] originated as the final rest area and supply hub for [[Alwari]] caravans crossing the [[Khorun Expanse]] east onto the [[Sorr-ul-Paan Plateau]] and [[Cuipernam]], or west towards the canyons of [[Delgerhan]]. The town is built at the foot of a cliff marked by a lone rock spire, serving since ancient times as a guiding marker for caravans traversing the expanse. Its very name is said to be the extinct local Alwari dialect's term for "great spire".
>
> While much fewer caravans traverse the planet today, Dashbalar continues to be the regions largest settlement and has preserved its role as the local supply and logistics hub. The town is also a favored trading center of the Alwari Qulun clan.
