> [!holonet] Public Holonet Entry
> An old cobalt mine located near the trading town of [[Dashbalar]]. Due to increasing offworld imports and decreasing ore  yields, it was abandoned a few cycles ago. Rumours speak of much larger deposits remaining at greater depths, and with the price of the mineral rising again, due to price-gouging from Imperial merchants, the local population has considered re-opening the mine. This currently seems unlikely, however, as bandits from [[Tormudai Fortress]] appear to have occupied the mine since.
