> [!holonet] Public Holonet Entry
> A decade ago, this riverport was a core component of the logistics infrastructure in and around the trade town of [[Dashbalar]], but changes to the natural water cycle of [[Ansion]] have diminished the local river significantly. While the change in water levels was initially compensated by dredging a channel along the river's center, this practice soon proved to be unviable over following cycles. As a result, the port has largely been abandoned today, and the cargo that was previously processed here is now being handled further downstream, from where it is then moved to Dashbalar overland.
